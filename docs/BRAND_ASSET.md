# Identitas visual Naraya Aurentis

Logo sumber pengguna: `frontend/assets/naraya-aurentis-logo.png`.

Logo transparan untuk aplikasi dan presentasi: `frontend/assets/naraya-aurentis-transparent.png`.

Adaptasi dibuat memakai tool imagegen bawaan. Gambar asli tetap disimpan. Versi transparan menyesuaikan latar dan merapikan halo agar menyatu dengan hijau gelap aplikasi. Hasil ini merupakan adaptasi raster, bukan master logo vektor resmi.

Prompt yang digunakan:

> Use case: background-extraction. Edit target: the supplied original Naraya Aurentis Monolithic logo. Create a clean, high-resolution transparent-background version for use inside a premium dental operations application. Preserve the exact distinctive interlocked angular mountain-like N/A geometric emblem, its dark charcoal faces and warm champagne-gold faces, the centered stacked composition and the exact brand wording: "NARAYA AURENTIS" and "MONOLITHIC". Preserve letter spacing, proportions and identity as faithfully as possible. Remove the rectangular teal/gray photographic background completely, including background between shapes. Remove the diffuse glow/halo for a crisp restrained professional finish. Gold outlines and lettering must be legible on deep forest charcoal #253630. Keep text straight, complete and accurate, with no substitutions. Use the complete mark with safe clear margin around all sides (do not crop the top). No added elements, no mockup, no scene, no new symbol or slogan. Deliver only the isolated full logo on actual alpha transparency.

Warna utama: hijau gelap `#253630`, hijau tindakan `#294C40`, emas `#AC8952`, latar hangat `#F4F4EF`. Jenis huruf aplikasi menggunakan Segoe UI dan Georgia pada bagian identitas. Presentasi mengikuti keluarga warna yang sama.
